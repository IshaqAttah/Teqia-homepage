Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what people ask most before reaching out.

Is Teqia's training really free?

Yes. Our tech training is free for the young people we serve, made possible by donors and volunteers who believe in our mission.

Can Teqia visit my school or community?

That's the heart of our work. Send us a message using “Invite Teqia to a school or community” and tell us a little about where you are.

How can I volunteer or mentor?

We'd love your help. Apply to become a mentor or volunteer, or send us a message about your skills and availability.

How can I or my organisation support Teqia?

You can make a donation or reach out about sponsorship and partnerships. All funds are applied solely to our mission.
