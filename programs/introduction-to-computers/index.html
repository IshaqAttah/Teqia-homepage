Novice Introduction to Computers

Introduction to Computers

The very beginning: what the parts of a computer do, switching one on and off safely, using a mouse and keyboard, saving your work where you can find it again, and typing without hunting for every letter.

Apply to learn this Fund this program
  • LevelNovice
  • How long6 weeks
  • CertificationPortfolio projects
  • Cost to youFree, always

Who this is for

You have never used a computer, or you use a phone but have never worked on a laptop. No experience is needed and nobody will make you feel behind.

What you will learn

  • Using a mouse and keyboard
  • Files and folders
  • Typing
  • Installing software
  • Looking after a machine

What we cover

  1. 1

    Weeks 1 to 2

    Getting started

    Parts of a computer, switching on and off safely, the desktop, windows, and using a mouse and keyboard.

    You finish with: Set up your own user account

  2. 2

    Weeks 3 to 4

    Files, folders and typing

    Saving and finding your work, organising folders, copying to a USB drive, and building typing speed.

    You finish with: A tidy folder of your own work

  3. 3

    Weeks 5 to 6

    Software and staying tidy

    Installing and removing programs, updates, storage, and what to do when something goes wrong.

    You finish with: Set up a machine from scratch

What you need

Time to study each week, and a laptop if you have one. If you do not have a laptop or steady internet, say so on your application. It will not count against you, and we will tell you what support we can offer.

Where this leads
