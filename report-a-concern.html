Safeguarding

Report a concern about a young person.

If you are worried that a young person is being harmed or is at risk, tell Teqia. You do not need an account, and you do not have to give your name.

If someone is in danger right now, do not use this form. Call the police on 112 or 199, or take them to the nearest hospital. Then tell Teqia on +234 902 330 2308.

Tell us what happened

Write as much or as little as you know. Something is better than nothing.

What is this about?

Pick whichever is closest. If none fit, choose the last one.

Your name

You do not have to give it. Teqia will look into the report either way. Leaving your name lets the safeguarding lead come back to you if something is unclear.

This goes to Teqia's safeguarding lead and nobody else. Not to mentors, not to the programme team. Every time the record is opened, Teqia notes who opened it.

What happens next

It reaches one person: Teqia's safeguarding lead. They aim to look at it within 24 hours, and the same day if it is serious.

Teqia may have to tell someone

If a child is at risk of serious harm, Teqia has to involve the authorities. Teqia will tell you if that happens, unless doing so would put someone in more danger.

Rather say it out loud?

Call +234 902 330 2308 and say you are reporting a safeguarding concern.
